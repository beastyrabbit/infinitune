## Scope and verification

- Public URL: https://infinitune.heerlab.com. The read-only kub-homelab manifest still pins web/server to 1.2.4. These pages describe the candidate, including the telemetry already merged on main, rather than claiming that live 1.2.4 contains it.
- Live inspection with agent-browser on Linux returned `Unauthorized` at `/`. No credentials were entered and no generation was triggered. The denial document contacted only `infinitune.heerlab.com`; document-visible cookie names, localStorage, sessionStorage and IndexedDB database names were empty. This **does not** establish the authenticated application's storage or HTTP-only cookies. A copied, authenticated Mac Chrome profile was not available in this Linux session.
- Code, deployment manifests and official public policy pages were inspected. No production database, credentials or secret values were read. Actual production provider/model settings, account privacy controls and recorder settings could not be established from the manifests. The shared facts were re-read after the netcup Art. 28 agreement was confirmed; that template statement is preserved.
- The public legal responses are full HTML with inline CSS, no scripts, fonts, API or external assets. nginx sends these paths through `location /` to the frontend on port 3000. No nginx edit is needed. The orchestrator must separately bypass Pangolin for exactly `/impressum`, `/datenschutz`, `/legal-notice`, `/privacy` and verify anonymous live access.
- No ads, donation buttons, paid features, payment SDKs or affiliate links were found in `apps/web/src`, `apps/web/public` or the server integrations. This supports the template's private, non-commercial description.

## Needs user decision

The following are publication blockers or privacy concerns, not statements that every possible optional feature is enabled. Each corresponding uncertainty remains visible as `TODO(user):` in both policy languages.

1. **SSO deletion and cookie:** establish deletion of Pocket-ID/Pangolin profiles including backups, plus exact session-cookie name, expiry and scope. Revoking access is not proof of erasure.
2. **Log retention:** verify Traefik's 14-day rotation before publication. Shared facts still call this IN PROGRESS. Confirm Pangolin HTTP 14/auth 90/network 30 days, backup limits, container logs and local CrowdSec alert/decision retention. Default Pino files persist across restarts without time expiry and may hold names, titles, URLs, peer IPs and error-path chat content.
3. **Application retention:** choose deletion criteria for users, playlists/prompts/songs, media/NAS copies, ratings/listening records, agent chats/memory/session files, devices, credentials and share-link records, including backups. No general automatic expiry or full account-erasure workflow was found. Ownership does not by itself imply isolation of global radio/settings data.
4. **Actual providers and transfers:** confirm production DB selections, credentials, custom Pi model endpoints, ACE/Ollama URLs and which model providers OpenRouter/Inference.sh actually use. Obtain relevant DPAs, full recipient addresses, account training/logging settings, retention and transfer safeguards. OpenAI official legal pages were blocked; no certification/address was guessed. Inference Shell's official page establishes only Delaware, not a street address. Shoo, LRCLIB, Bandcamp, DuckDuckGo, Wikimedia and arbitrary agent-fetched destinations still have unresolved address/safeguard details. A provider policy mentioning SCCs does not establish a signed agreement for this account.
5. **Telemetry proportionality/consent:** decide whether analytics and 100%-eligible session replay/heatmaps are needed for an invited family tool and whether consent is required. Cookie-free analytics and strict masking do not alone settle GDPR or terminal-access requirements. No banner/consent controls exist. Establish actual recording policy and analytics/error/trace/backup retention. Exception scrubbing cannot reliably remove free-text personal content. The legal pages themselves contain no browser telemetry.
6. **Research/source services:** decide whether to keep LRCLIB, public web research and arbitrary agent fetches with unverified recipients/transfers. Prompts can contain personal data that may enter generated search queries or content. Reference downloads also require a separate licensing assessment; do not represent server-side fetching as a browser embed or consented third-party access.
7. **GlitchTip SMTP:** determine whether alert emails are active, precisely what error content goes through Resend and its retention, DPA and onward recipients. The SMTP manifest establishes configuration, not that a particular email was sent.
8. **CrowdSec:** confirm Art. 26 arrangements for the joint processing described by CrowdSec. Do not claim an agreement has been verified or processing is EU-only. Automated attack blocking is described separately from musical preference adaptation and the template's Art. 22 statement.
9. **Delivery prerequisites owned by orchestrator:** contact form is not yet live; aliases are confirmed. Re-read shared readiness facts, reconcile any new privacy-relevant main changes, and verify anonymous public access after the exact Pangolin bypass is installed. Do not publish unresolved TODO text as a finalized policy.
